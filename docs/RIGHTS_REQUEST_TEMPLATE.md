# Rights request template

Use this only after identifying the correct legal entity and contact. Replace every bracketed field. Keep the first message short; attach technical detail only when requested.

---

**Subject:** Non-commercial preservation demonstration request — *Harry Potter: Magic Awakened*

Hello [RIGHTS OR PRESERVATION TEAM],

I maintain an independent preservation record for *Harry Potter: Magic Awakened*. The public project currently documents client identity, release history, player-experience capture methods, and provenance. It does not distribute the game client, extracted assets, credentials, private player data, or proprietary server code.

I am asking whether [ORGANIZATION] would consider authorizing a limited, non-commercial demonstration for preservation and educational use.

The proposed first exhibit would be deliberately narrow:

- [ON-SITE / PRIVATE EVALUATION / PUBLIC WEB] access;
- [NUMBER] simultaneous users;
- [TERM OR EXHIBITION DATES];
- one approved client or exhibit build;
- one bounded exploration area;
- [ONE TUTORIAL / ONE SCRIPTED DUEL / OTHER APPROVED MECHANICS];
- no purchases, advertising, public chat, competitive ranking, or migration of live accounts;
- no claim that the exhibit is an official or continuing service;
- only the assets and marks explicitly listed in an agreed content schedule.

The project can provide:

- cryptographic provenance for preserved inputs;
- strict separation of licensed materials from project-authored code;
- private technical review before publication;
- geographic, audience, concurrency, time, and access restrictions;
- an agreed takedown and correction process;
- privacy controls and no collection of payment credentials;
- attribution and notices in your required form;
- deposit with an agreed archive, museum, library, or university if useful.

Could you tell me:

1. whether [ORGANIZATION] controls any rights needed for this proposal;
2. which other entities should be included;
3. whether you would review a two-page exhibit specification;
4. what restrictions or alternative preservation arrangement you would prefer?

Public project: https://kvandre12-commits.github.io/hpma-preservation/
Source and methodology: https://github.com/kvandre12-commits/hpma-preservation

I understand that no permission exists unless it is granted in writing, and that approval from one participant may not cover franchise, software, music, performance, privacy, middleware, or distribution rights controlled by others.

Thank you for considering a preservation pathway.

## Before sending

- Verify the recipient and legal entity.
- Replace every bracketed field.
- Do not attach the APK, extracted assets, credentials, traffic captures, or private player records.
- Do not claim imminent shutdown unless an official source establishes it.
- Keep a dated copy of the request and response.
- Ask permission before publishing correspondence.
- Have counsel review any agreement before accepting obligations or releasing a demonstration.
