01 / Purpose

Games are more than executables.

They are places, performances, communities, interfaces, music, stories, and shared memories. A client backup matters, but it cannot preserve a live service alone. This project records what exists, what is missing, and what permission would be needed to host a faithful demonstration later.

04 / Future demonstration

Ask first. Scope tightly. Host honestly.

Our preferred outcome is cooperation: permission to build a limited, non-commercial demonstration that preserves representative mechanics and cultural context without impersonating the official service.

  • No APK or extracted commercial assets are distributed here.
  • No credentials, private chats, payment data, or authentication tokens are collected.
  • No claim of affiliation with Warner Bros., Portkey Games, Wizarding World, NetEase, or their licensors is made.
  • Any future playable exhibit remains gated on written rights and a documented content scope.
